Will it rain tomorrow?

ModelAccuracy on 2015
Always say no rain60.4%
Tomorrow = today70.3%
Logistic regression73.1%
Bayesian logistic regression73.1%
Linear SVM72.5%
RBF SVM72.0%
Honest reading: the best models gain about 3 points over "tomorrow = today". Today's weather already holds most of what can be known about tomorrow's rain.
