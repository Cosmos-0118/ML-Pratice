The data

One dataset for every week

One row per day: rain in millimetres, the highest and lowest temperature in °C, and the wind speed. Four years, no missing values, one small CSV file.

1,461days, one row each
4 years2012 – 2015
43%of days have rain (precipitation > 0)
2015held out as the test year

Source: NOAA National Climatic Data Center, as packaged in the vega-datasets collection (U.S. Government work, public domain). Every model is trained on 2012–2014 and tested on 2015, so nothing ever sees the future.
