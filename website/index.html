→5

The data

One small dataset for everything

One row per day: rain in millimetres, the highest and lowest temperature, and the wind speed. No missing values, one small CSV file.

1,461days, one row each
2012–2015four full years
5columns: rain, two temperatures, wind, weather type
2015always held out as the test year

Source: NOAA National Climatic Data Center, as packaged in vega-datasets (U.S. Government work, public domain). Models are trained on 2012–2014 and tested on 2015, so nothing ever sees the future.
