Practice tasks covered
  • Practice 1 – implement a hidden Markov model to predict sequential data (the daily rain sequence)
  • Each day is dry, light rain or heavy rain. A Markov chain says tomorrow depends on today; a hidden Markov model adds a hidden weather regime that we never see.
  • The forward algorithm is checked against brute force, and Baum–Welch (EM) learns the model from the data alone.
  • BIC picks 2 hidden states.
RegimeDryLight rainHeavy rainWindAverage high
Wet spell19%44%37%3.612.7 °C
Dry spell93%7%0%2.919.9 °C
The regimes are real: wind and temperature were never given to the model, yet the wet spell is windier and cooler. Part of that gap is just winter being wetter than summer.
Honest reading: predicting tomorrow, the HMM gets 68.7%, the Markov chain 70.3% and "tomorrow = today" 70.3%. A hidden regime adds little over looking at today.
